sd-graph engine test bed

Not course notes and not for publication. These configs are fixtures that exercise every shape the engine draws, with made-up numbers, so a change to engine/sd-graph.js can be eyeballed before it reaches a notes file. Real widgets take their numbers from the source material only.

1. Symbolic single shift (preset)

2. Schedule plus curve (numeric, straight line, hollow dots)

3. Schedule shift with scenarios

4. Surplus and shortage (guides, brace, arrows)

5. Two panels (two markets), curved curves with numbered markers

6. Vertical brace and a movement-along arrow
