ECO2013 Graph Widgets: Images 1–20

Every static graph from the first half of the Supply and Demand notes, rebuilt as a widget that follows the conventions of the printed artwork: curved demand and supply on conceptual graphs, straight lines on numeric ones, numbered markers for "Point 1 / Point 2", black for the original curve and red for the shifted one, dashed gridlines on schedule graphs, and a red price line with a brace for surpluses and shortages. Each widget is a short JSON block; the drawing code is shared.
